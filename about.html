Dong-Sup Yoon

20th President, Yonsei University

Yonsei University has always followed the righteous path through the twists and turns of Korea's modern history. As a distinguished global academic institution, Yonsei has made significant contributions to society and humanity. Inheriting this prestigious tradition, we aim to advance into a new era of Yonsei, thereby opening the gateway to the future with what makes us uniquely YONSEI.

In the age of "Big Blur," we are experiencing the collapse of boundaries between academia and industry. We are confronted with the dual challenges of digital transformation and energy transition on the fiercely competitive battlefield of innovation. Words like creativity, consilience, and convergence have long since become keywords in our society. Innovation is the only way to stay relevant in this rapidly changing international environment.

Yonsei's enduring mission for over 140 years has been to nurture talents who drive innovation and change. We will continue to build on this foundation, cultivating individuals who proactively tackle the problems of global society while pursuing the values of coexistence.

Our Guiding Philosophy

Yonsei University serves as the "alma mater" of all arts and sciences to nurture leaders who will contribute to Korean and international society, in the ecumenical spirit of Christian teaching epitomized in its motto of "truth and freedom." Yonseians will inherit mankind's cultural legacy, and lead academic development through creativity and critical thinking.

We serve others with an open heart and contribute to the prosperity of humankind. With these missions, Yonseians demonstrate leadership to realize the Yonsei spirit globally. Our interdisciplinary research initiative is rooted in a humanistic understanding of humanity and nature embedded in Yonsei spirit.

With a focus on future sustainability — including artificial intelligence, biotechnology, and renewable energy — Yonsei will seek to elevate global research competitiveness. We will strengthen programs that foster creative future talent, such as the "Student-Designed Semester," enabling students to freely explore academia and grow as independent agents of the future society.

Our Heritage

140 Years of History

From a small Christian mission school in 1885 to one of Asia's foremost research universities, Yonsei's story is inseparable from Korea's own modern transformation.

1885

Founding of Gwanghyewon

American missionary Horace N. Allen established Gwanghyewon, Korea's first modern hospital and medical school. This institution became the seed from which Severance Union Medical College would grow, eventually forming one half of Yonsei University.

1915

Chosun Christian College Established

American missionary Horace G. Underwood founded the Chosun Christian College — the first modern higher education institution in Korea — laying the academic foundation for what would become Yonsei University. The Department of Commerce, established in the same year, is the oldest business school in Korea.

1917

Severance Union Medical College

Severance Union Medical College was formally established, consolidating the legacy of modern medical education in Korea and cementing Yonsei's commitment to health sciences that continues through the world-renowned Severance Hospital today.

1946

Yonhi College Becomes a University

Chosun Christian College was renamed Yonhi University, marking the expansion of its academic programs and its role in rebuilding Korean society following the liberation from Japanese colonial rule.

1957

Merger — Birth of Yonsei University

Yonhi University and Severance Union Medical College merged to form Yonsei University — a name combining "Yonhi" and "Severance" — creating one of Korea's most comprehensive research universities with deep roots in both the liberal arts and medical sciences.

2010

Songdo International Campus Opens

Yonsei established its International Campus in Songdo, Incheon — a state-of-the-art residential campus where all freshmen (except medical and dental students) complete their first year, experiencing interdisciplinary liberal arts education in a diverse, global environment.

2024

Vision: Yonsei 150

Under the leadership of 20th President Dong-Sup Yoon, Yonsei launched its ambitious "Empower! Be YONSEI!" vision — charting a course toward the university's 150th anniversary in 2035, with a focus on AI, biotechnology, renewable energy, and interdisciplinary global leadership.

Campus Network

Our Campuses

Yonsei operates two major campuses in Korea, each serving distinct academic missions while sharing a unified commitment to excellence and innovation.

Main Campus

Sinchon, Seoul

The heart of Yonsei University, located in the Seodaemun district of Seoul. Home to 14 colleges and 15 graduate schools, the Sinchon campus spans a beautiful, historic campus featuring iconic buildings including the landmark Underwood Hall. The campus houses over 32,000 students and more than 150 research centers.

International Campus

Songdo, Incheon

Opened in 2010 within the Songdo International Business District, this state-of-the-art residential campus serves as the first-year home for all incoming undergraduate students (excluding medical, dental, and music majors). The campus offers 5 colleges and an immersive liberal arts residential college experience.

MIRAE Campus

Wonju, Gangwon

The MIRAE (Future) Campus in Wonju operates as Yonsei's regional campus, offering 4 colleges, 2 divisions, and 3 graduate schools. With approximately 7,700 students and its own medical school — Wonju College of Medicine — the campus extends Yonsei's academic reach beyond Seoul.
