Featured Program

Underwood International College (UIC)

Underwood International College is a highly selective, all-English, four-year liberal arts institution that combines the intimate, elite learning environment of a liberal arts college with the faculty and resources of a top research university in Korea.

UIC was established in 2005 in memory of Horace G. Underwood, the American missionary who founded Yonsei University in 1915. It offers a rigorous interdisciplinary curriculum taught entirely in English, attracting outstanding students from around the world. UIC graduates are known for their critical thinking, global perspective, and capacity for leadership across diverse fields.

Students may choose from divisions including Humanities, Social Sciences, Science & Technology, and more — all within a liberal arts framework that encourages cross-disciplinary exploration.

Advanced Study

Graduate Education

Yonsei operates 15 graduate schools on the main campus alone, offering master's and doctoral degrees across every academic discipline. The university's General Graduate School and professional graduate schools provide pathways to advanced expertise and research leadership.

General Graduate School

Offering master's and doctoral programs across all colleges and departments. As of 2024, the main campus has 8,862 master's students and 3,604 doctoral students. Combined and accelerated (BS-MS) programs are also available. Doctoral programs include combined MS-PhD tracks for accelerated research training.

Professional Graduate Schools

Yonsei offers a range of professional and specialized graduate schools including the Graduate School of Business (MBA), Graduate School of Law, Graduate School of Public Administration, Graduate School of International Studies, and Graduate School of Education, among others.

Non-Degree Programs

For international students and visiting scholars, Yonsei offers comprehensive non-degree programs including credit exchange, language courses through the world-renowned Korean Language Institute (KLI), and research residencies. Over 1,485 students annually participate in language programs through the KLI alone.

Innovation in Education

Student-Designed Semester

As part of Yonsei's forward-looking academic vision, the Student-Designed Semester program empowers students to chart their own academic journeys — blending disciplines, pursuing independent projects, and developing the creative problem-solving skills demanded by the future.

Empower! Be YONSEI!

Under President Yoon's vision, Yonsei is strengthening programs that foster creative future talent. The Student-Designed Semester enables students to freely explore academia, growing as independent agents of the future society — combining coursework with entrepreneurship, research, community engagement, and global study in ways that no fixed curriculum can offer. This initiative reflects Yonsei's belief that the most important education is the one each student designs for themselves.
