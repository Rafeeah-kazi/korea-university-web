Admissions Overview

Why Yonsei?

A University with 140 Years of Excellence

Yonsei University is one of Korea's most prestigious universities and a globally recognized research institution. Whether you are a Korean national, an overseas Korean, or an international student from anywhere in the world, Yonsei offers a transformative education rooted in Christian values of truth and freedom — combined with cutting-edge research, world-class faculty, and a vibrant student life in the heart of Seoul.

Our undergraduate programs span 14 colleges across the humanities, sciences, engineering, business, medicine, and the arts. Graduate study is available through 15 graduate schools offering master's, doctoral, and combined programs. For international students, Yonsei also offers a range of non-degree programs, credit exchange opportunities, and the world-renowned Korean Language Institute (KLI).

Located in Sinchon, one of Seoul's most energetic and culturally rich neighborhoods, the Yonsei campus places students at the intersection of Korea's academic excellence and its dynamic modern culture. Seoul is consistently ranked among the world's top cities for students, and Yonsei sits at the very heart of it.

Application Process

How to Apply

The application process varies slightly between undergraduate, graduate, and non-degree programs, but all international applicants follow a clear, structured path. Here is the general process for undergraduate international applicants.

01

Choose Your Program

Research Yonsei's 14 undergraduate colleges and select the degree program that aligns with your academic background, interests, and career goals. Review language requirements and any program-specific criteria.

02

Prepare Your Documents

Gather required materials: academic transcripts (officially translated), graduation certificates, language proficiency scores (TOPIK for Korean-medium programs, TOEFL/IELTS for English programs), personal statement, and letters of recommendation.

03

Submit Your Application

Applications are submitted online through Yonsei's admissions portal. Ensure all documents are complete and uploaded before the published deadline — late or incomplete applications will not be reviewed.

04

Visa & Enrollment

Admitted students must apply for the appropriate student visa (D-2 for degree-seeking students). The university's Global Students Center (GOSC) provides full visa issuance support and orientation for incoming international students.

Financial Support

Scholarships & Financial Aid

Yonsei University is committed to making a world-class education accessible. The university awarded over 144 billion KRW in scholarships in 2023 alone — covering merit scholarships, need-based aid, international student grants, and government-funded programs.

Merit Scholarships

Awarded to students with exceptional academic performance. Merit scholarships at Yonsei can cover a substantial portion of tuition and are reviewed each semester based on GPA and academic standing.

Up to 100% tuition

Need-Based Aid

Students demonstrating financial need may apply for need-based scholarships and emergency financial support. Applications are assessed through the Office of Student Affairs with consideration for family income and circumstances.

Partial to full tuition

International Student Scholarships

Yonsei offers dedicated scholarships for international students, including the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS / KGSP) funded by the Korean government, as well as Yonsei's own international admissions scholarships for outstanding applicants.

Tuition + living allowance

Research Assistantships

Graduate students engaged in research may qualify for research assistantships (RA) or teaching assistantships (TA) funded by faculty grants or university research budgets, providing a stipend and tuition waiver in exchange for research contributions.

Stipend + tuition waiver

Total Scholarships Awarded (2023): Main Campus — 114,931 million KRW | MIRAE Campus — 29,303 million KRW. Contact the Office of Scholarships for current scholarship categories, application cycles, and eligibility requirements.

Visa Information

Student Visa Guidance

All international students studying at Yonsei on a degree or long-term non-degree basis require a valid Korean student visa. The Yonsei Global Students Center (GOSC) provides comprehensive support for visa issuance, from document preparation to post-arrival registration.

The primary visa types for Yonsei students are the D-2 (Academic Sojourner) for degree-seeking students and D-4 (Language Training) for those attending the Korean Language Institute. Visiting scholars typically hold D-2 or E-1 (Professor) visas depending on their engagement.

Yonsei's visa support team assists with Certificate of Admission issuance, document authentication, alien registration, and any changes to visa status during enrollment. Students are strongly encouraged to begin the visa process as soon as they receive their admission letter.

D-2 Visa — Academic Sojourner

For degree-seeking students (undergraduate, master's, doctoral). Required for all international students enrolled in a degree program at Yonsei.

D-4 Visa — Language Training

For students attending the Korean Language Institute (KLI) or other non-degree language programs. May be converted to a D-2 if the student later enrolls in a degree program.

Credit Exchange Students

Students arriving via official partner university agreements may be eligible for a C-3 (Short-term) or D-2 visa depending on the length and nature of their exchange. Consult GOSC for guidance.

For Visiting Scholars

International scholars and postdoctoral researchers should consult Yonsei's Office of International Affairs (GOSC) for E-1 or D-2 visa eligibility based on their research appointment terms.
