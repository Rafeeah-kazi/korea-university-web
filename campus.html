Student Life

A Campus Built for Discovery

More Than a Place to Study

Life at Yonsei is about far more than lectures and exams. The Sinchon campus — set against the backdrop of Ansan Mountain in the heart of western Seoul — is one of Korea's most beloved university environments. Its tree-lined boulevards, historic buildings, and vibrant plazas make it both a place of serious scholarship and joyful living.

Students at Yonsei benefit from a comprehensive ecosystem of support, culture, and opportunity. From student-run clubs and societies to state-of-the-art sports facilities, academic support centers, counseling services, and cultural events — the campus is designed to help every student thrive, not just academically, but as whole people.

The Yonsei student community is notably international. With over 6,375 international students from across the globe — degree-seeking, credit exchange, and language program students alike — the campus is a genuinely multicultural environment where global friendships and perspectives are part of everyday life.

Student Self-Governing Activities

Yonsei has a long tradition of robust student governance. Student associations at the university, college, and department levels organize academic events, cultural programs, welfare initiatives, and social activities throughout the year.

Akaraka — Yonsei's Legendary Festival

Akaraka is Yonsei's annual open-air music and culture festival, one of the most famous university festivals in Korea, traditionally featuring headline performances from Korea's biggest music acts and attended by tens of thousands.

Yon-Ko Games (연고전)

The annual Yon-Ko sporting competition between Yonsei University and Korea University is one of Korea's most anticipated collegiate sporting events — a multi-day rivalry festival celebrating school pride, community, and athletic excellence across football, basketball, baseball, hockey, and rugby.

Residential College

Living & Learning Together

All incoming Yonsei freshmen (with the exception of students in medicine, dentistry, and music) spend their first year at the Songdo International Campus in Incheon as part of the Residential College (RC) program. This unique, immersive experience creates a tight-knit learning community where students from every major live, eat, and learn together.

The RC program combines structured academic programming with dormitory life, community service, cultural activities, and leadership development. Students are grouped into RC communities led by Resident Assistants (RAs) and faculty RC Fellows who guide their academic and personal development throughout the year.

This first-year experience is intentionally interdisciplinary — designed to broaden students' horizons before they dive deep into their chosen major, and to forge lifelong friendships across disciplines, backgrounds, and nationalities.

RC Program Highlights

  • All freshmen (non-medical/dental/music) participate
  • Located at Songdo International Campus, Incheon
  • Faculty RC Fellows guide student development
  • Interdisciplinary academic programming
  • Community service & cultural activities
  • Fosters cross-disciplinary peer networks
  • 24/7 residential support & counseling

On-Campus Housing

Dormitories & Residences

Yonsei's main campus offers on-campus housing for over 10,000 students, with facilities designed to support both study and personal well-being in the heart of Seoul.

International House

Designed specifically to support international students, the International House provides furnished rooms, Korean language resources, cultural exchange programming, and a welcoming community that helps new arrivals settle into life in Seoul and at Yonsei.

Primary housing for international students

SK Global House

A modern, purpose-built dormitory offering premium facilities for both Korean and international students. SK Global House features study rooms, fitness facilities, common areas, and a design that encourages cross-cultural interaction and academic collaboration.

Mixed Korean & international residential community

Muak Dormitory

One of Yonsei's established residential buildings, Muak Dormitory offers comfortable on-campus accommodation close to academic buildings, the library, and campus facilities. Its central location makes it popular with upperclass and graduate students.

Central campus location — all student levels

Songdo RC Residences

At the Songdo International Campus, the Residential College provides dedicated dormitory spaces as part of the first-year RC experience. Housing at Songdo is integrated with academic programming, dining halls, recreational facilities, and community spaces across a 58,738 m² residential footprint.

Capacity: 4,280 students (MIRAE/Songdo)

Main Campus Housing: 239,471 m² of residential space with a total capacity of 10,012 students — one of the largest on-campus residential capacities among Korean universities. Housing applications are coordinated through the university's Office of Student Affairs.

Campus Facilities

World-Class Campus Infrastructure

From sports complexes and health centers to libraries, cafeterias, and cultural venues, the Yonsei campus provides everything a student needs to live, study, and flourish.

Health & Sports Facilities

Yonsei's sports infrastructure includes the Sports Science Complex, swimming pools, tennis courts, football pitches, an indoor gymnasium, and a dedicated fitness center. These facilities serve students, faculty, and staff, and host competitive intercollegiate sports.

Yonsei University Library

One of Korea's largest academic library systems, Yonsei's libraries hold millions of volumes and provide comprehensive digital research databases, study spaces, group rooms, and 24-hour access during exam periods. The Central Library is a landmark building at the heart of the Sinchon campus.

Dining & Stores

The Yonsei campus features numerous cafeterias, restaurants, cafés, and convenience facilities managed through the university and by student-serving private vendors. Multiple dining halls offer diverse Korean and international menus at student-friendly prices throughout the day.

Campus Health Center

Students have access to on-campus medical and counseling services. In addition to primary care and pharmacy services on campus, students benefit from Yonsei's proximity to Severance Hospital — one of Korea's premier university hospitals — for more specialized medical needs.

Cultural & Event Venues

The campus hosts several venues for lectures, performances, exhibitions, and ceremonies, including the Yonsei University Auditorium and multiple open-air spaces that serve as the backdrop for Yonsei's famous festivals including Akaraka and the Yon-Ko Games celebrations.

Transportation & Parking

The Sinchon campus is easily accessible via the Seoul Metro (Sinchon Station, Line 2), buses, and campus shuttle services. Parking information and venue rental/reservation services are available through the university's administrative offices for students, faculty, and visiting guests.

Getting Around

Transportation

The Yonsei Sinchon campus is superbly connected to the rest of Seoul and Korea, making it easy for students, faculty, and visitors to navigate their daily commute and weekend explorations.

Seoul Metro — Line 2

Sinchon Station (Line 2) is located directly adjacent to the Sinchon campus, connecting Yonsei to downtown Seoul, major transit hubs, and the rest of the city within minutes.

Campus Shuttle

Internal campus shuttles operate between key buildings, dormitories, and the library — particularly useful on the expansive main campus and during inclement weather.

Bus Network

Multiple city bus lines serve the Yonsei area, connecting the campus to all major districts of Seoul including Hongdae, Mapo, Jongno, and Gangnam.

Songdo Campus

The Songdo International Campus is accessible from Seoul via the Incheon Subway (Incheon Line 1), making the first-year RC experience easily connected to Seoul's urban amenities.
