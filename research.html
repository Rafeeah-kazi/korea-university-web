Research Overview

A Global Research Powerhouse

Leading Innovation Across Every Discipline

Yonsei University's research enterprise spans the full arc of human inquiry — from fundamental science and the humanities to cutting-edge engineering and clinical medicine. Our researchers are published in the world's leading journals, cited by scholars globally, and partnered with leading corporations, government agencies, and international institutions.

The university has made a strategic commitment to four areas of future research leadership: artificial intelligence and digital transformation, biotechnology and the life sciences, renewable energy and sustainability, and interdisciplinary approaches to global social challenges. These pillars reflect both Yonsei's existing strengths and the most urgent questions facing humanity today.

Yonsei's research infrastructure includes the Elsevier PURE Researchers Portal, which provides a comprehensive, searchable database of all faculty publications, grants, and research outputs — enabling transparency and fostering collaboration with partners worldwide.

Key Research Areas

Areas of Research Excellence

Yonsei's researchers are advancing knowledge across a wide spectrum of disciplines, with particular strength in these areas of global impact.

AI & Machine Learning

The College of Computing — home to dedicated AI and Computer Science programs — is producing world-class research in deep learning, AI ethics, autonomous systems, and intelligent semiconductors. Yonsei's AI research benefits from close industry partnerships and interdisciplinary collaboration across engineering, medicine, and the social sciences.

Biomedical Science

The College of Life Science & Biotechnology leads research in systems biology, biochemistry, and applied biotechnology. Working in close synergy with Severance Hospital, Yonsei researchers translate fundamental discoveries into clinical breakthroughs — addressing cancer, aging, rare diseases, and precision medicine.

Energy & Environment

With dedicated programs in Battery Engineering, Chemical Engineering, and Atmospheric Sciences, Yonsei is at the forefront of research into next-generation batteries, carbon capture, climate modeling, and sustainable materials — essential contributions to Korea's and the world's energy transition.

Social Sciences & Humanities

Yonsei's humanistic research tradition — rooted in philosophy, history, political science, and theology — enriches and contextualizes scientific research. The university is committed to ensuring that technological progress serves human dignity, social justice, and international cooperation.

Clinical Medicine

The Yonsei University Health System, anchored by Severance Hospital, Gangnam Severance Hospital, and Wonju Severance Christian Hospital, is one of Korea's most comprehensive academic health systems. Clinical research across oncology, cardiology, neurology, and surgery integrates directly with Yonsei's graduate training programs.

Basic Sciences

From astrophysics and pure mathematics to particle physics and atmospheric modeling, Yonsei's College of Science pursues knowledge for its own sake — generating fundamental insights that underpin applied research across every field. The College hosts dedicated departments in Astronomy, Atmospheric Sciences, and Earth System Sciences.

Yonsei University Health System

Severance Hospital & Clinical Research

Asia's Premier Academic Health System

The Yonsei University Health System (YUHS) is one of Korea's largest and most prestigious academic medical systems, comprising Severance Hospital in Sinchon, Gangnam Severance Hospital, and Wonju Severance Christian Hospital. Together, these institutions serve over two million patients annually while training the next generation of physicians and conducting cutting-edge clinical research.

Severance Hospital — founded in 1885 as Korea's first modern hospital by American missionary Horace N. Allen — holds a unique place in Korean medical history. Today it operates at the forefront of organ transplantation, cancer treatment, cardiovascular surgery, and precision medicine, with facilities ranked among the best in Asia.

Clinical research at YUHS is directly integrated into Yonsei's graduate medical programs. Physician-scientists at the College of Medicine, College of Dentistry, College of Nursing, and College of Pharmacy collaborate with clinical faculty to design and conduct trials that translate laboratory discoveries into life-saving treatments.
